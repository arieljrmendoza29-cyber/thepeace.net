About the Firm

A Texas-rooted firm with a national investment discipline.

Founded in 2021 and headquartered in Arlington, Texas, ThePeace.net brings institutional-grade rigor to the acquisition and management of residential and commercial real estate.

Our Story

Peace of mind, by design.

ThePeace.net was established in 2021 with a specific conviction: real estate investing works best when it is disciplined, transparent, and built to last. We specialize in identifying, acquiring, and managing high-yield residential and commercial property assets across strategic growth markets.

By combining rigorous market analysis with strategic capital deployment, we help our investors build long-term wealth while providing high-quality housing solutions to the communities we operate in. It's a two-sided commitment — returns for our partners, stability for our neighborhoods.

Our Philosophy

Every property we acquire has to earn its place in the portfolio. Every deal we underwrite has to answer to conservative assumptions. Every investor we partner with deserves to see the numbers we see, when we see them.

That's the standard. It hasn't changed since day one.

Where We Operate

Our headquarters in Arlington, Texas anchors our operational base, but our acquisition strategy extends to residential and commercial opportunities across high-growth corridors — with an emphasis on markets where fundamentals support durable long-term appreciation.
